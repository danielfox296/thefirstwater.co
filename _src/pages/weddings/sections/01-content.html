Three ways to book

The bachelorette, the weekend, or the two of you

The bachelorette, from $450

One space, one session, for the bride and her people, at the house you rented or a partner space in Denver. Everyone lies down in low light under blankets, and for seventy-five minutes nobody has to talk. It fits the afternoon before the big night out, or the morning after it.

The wedding weekend

A session built into the weekend: the welcome evening, the morning of, or the day after for whoever stayed. Your people lie down together before the day starts or after it ends, and I open and close it with ceremony the way I do every session.

The two of you

A private session for the couple, the week of the wedding or the week after. You share the same seventy-five minutes of sound side by side, and you leave with somewhere to start talking. Couples Reconnection is the public version of this night, and this one is yours alone.
